Radioactive Ammo Radioactive Ammo is a powerful Item that causes player projectiles split into multiple, weaker projectiles when destroyed. Notes
  • The damage delt by its aura is relative to the center position of enemies. The closer the center position of the enemy is to the projectiles center, the more damage it deals every tick.
