Shrapnel Shrapnel is a powerful item that causes parried projectiles split into multiple, weaker projectiles the mimic player projectiles when hitting an enemy. Notes
  • Parrying your own projectiles can be extremely powerful while having Shrapnel, creating an effect that closely resembles Cluster Ammo.
Synergies
  • Items that let player projectiles hit enemies multiple times like Screw, Drill Ammo, and Ghost Ammo are very powerful while having Shrapnel since projectiles get created every time an enemy is hit with a parried projectile, including drill ticks.
  • Boxing Gloves, Shield, Extendo Grip and Reinforced Shield all increase the players melee size, which can make it easier to parry their own projectiles.
