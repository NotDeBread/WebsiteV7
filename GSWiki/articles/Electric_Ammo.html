Electric Ammo Elecrtic Ammo is a strong Item that causes player projectiles to create 'arcs' that damage nearby enemies when hitting an enemy. The arcs max range is defined by the players electricChainReach value plus 100 in pixels. Notes
  • Each enemy can only be arced to once, meaning an arc can't repeatedly go between two enemies.
