Sasha (Miniboss) This article is about the Miniboss not to be confused with the Character, see Sasha. Sasha was one of the first bosses introduced to Goober Shooter 2 in playtest15. She is a very fast-paced boss that deals no contact damage and fires bursts of projectiles. Behavior Phase 1 During phase 1, Sasha can either dash towards the player or create a ring of high-damage projectiles. Phase 2 (enraged) Changes
  • playtest17: Introduced Sasha's second phase. At the time being the only boss with phases.
  • playtest17: Raised Sasha's HP from 500 to 750.
