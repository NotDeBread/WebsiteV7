Ghost Ammo Ghost Ammo is a strong Item that allows projectiles to hit an enemy many times at the cost of damage. Synergies
  • Parrying a player projectile while having Ghost Ammo and Shrapnel can create many player projectiles.
  • Golden Ammo with Ghost Ammo can give player projectiles multiple chances to spawn a coin, since it triggers every time it hits an enemy.
  • Ghost Ammo gives Sharp Ammo more chances to apply bleeding to enemies.
  • Black Feather with Ghost Ammo allows player projectiles to gain damage exponentially very quickly.
Trivia
  • The texture for Ghost Ammo is slightly transparent.
