FRIEND Gameplay FRIEND has no projectile based weapon, but instead creates a trail of poison fields the mimic player projectile attributes, similar to the ones created by the Drool item.
Them not having a projectile based weapon can make it really hard to deal with enemies with poison fields like Mutants or Monstrosities. To combat this, they start with an increased maxWeaponDistance stat. Allowing the player to melee these out of reach enemies. Unlocking When entering the Art Room in the Credits, there is a 1 in 50 chance the player will be put into the Egg Room. Interacting with the man behind the tree completes the The Egg achievement, unlocking FRIEND
Interacting with the man behind the tree triggers this dialogue:
  • "Well, there is a man here."
  • "You glance up at them. The leaves of the tree are covering his face."
  • "The man gently grabs your wrist and places something in your palm."
  • "You look down towards your hand."
  • "It's an egg."
  • "You look up at the man."
  • "But there was nobody there."
Unlock Requirement Obtain the egg from the man behind the tree.
