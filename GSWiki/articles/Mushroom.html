Mushroom Chalk is an Item that increases a random player stat. Increasing some stats can be negative.
The stats that can be affected are:
  • bullet.damage (+)
  • bullet.speed (+)
  • bullet.size (+)
  • bullet.shotCooldown (-)
  • bullet.range (+)
  • player.maxHealth (+)
  • ammo.reloadSpeed (-)
  • ammo.max (+)
  • melee.size (+)
  • melee.damage (+)
When buying this item, the player has a 80% chance for the effect to be positive.
