Pickups Pickups are entities that spawn in the area that trigger something once collided with. Currently, there are 5 different kinds of pickups: Coins Coins are the most common kind of pickup, each wave spawning coins with value equal to the current wave. There are 5 different coins that can spawn, each with thier own values:
  • Copper Coin: $1
  • Silver Coin: $5
  • Gold Coin: $10
  • Emerald Coin: $25
  • Diamond Coin: $100
Battery The battery is a rare pickup, only being able to spawn from the Pocket Change power item. It gives +25 POWER. Candies Candies are small pickups introduced in Playtest26 - The Spooky Update which give random effects:
  • Health Candy (20%)
    • Gives +10 HP
  • Red Candy (18%)
    • Gives the Strength Status Effect for 4 seconds.
    • If the player already has the Strength Status Effect, it will increase its duration by 4 seconds instead.
  • Blue Candy (18%)
    • Gives the Buff Status Effect for 4 seconds.
    • If the player already has the Buff Status Effect, it will increase its duration by 4 seconds instead.
  • Ammo Candy (18%)
    • Reloads the players weapon.
    • This can only be picked up if the player is not at max ammo.
  • Scary Candy (5%)
    • Gives the Scary Status Effect for 2 seconds.
    • If the player already has the Scary Status Effect, it will increase its duration by 2 seconds instead.
  • Electric Candy (14%)
    • Gives the Statically Charged Status Effect for 6 seconds.
    • If the player already has the Statically Charged Status Effect, it will increase its duration by 6 seconds instead.
  • Projectile Candy (18%)
    • Spawns a projectile identical to a player projectile facing towards the nearest enemy.
    • If there are no enemies, it will face in a random direction instead.
  • Golden Candy (5%)
    • Gives a random amount of money between 25 and 100.
  • Rainbow Candy (4%)
    • Gives a random stat up.
    • All possible stat ups have an equal chance to be chosen.
    • The possible stat ups are as follows:
      • player.speed + 0.1
      • player.maxHealth + 10
      • player.maxPower + 1
      • player.grazeSize + 0.1
      • player.parryHeal + 1
      • shop.luck + 0.5
      • shop.rerolls + 1
      • bullet.damage + 1
      • bullet.shotCooldown * 0.95
      • bullet.range + 5
      • bullet.critChance + 5
      • bullet.critDamageMult + 0.1
      • bullet.drillChance + 1
      • bullet.bounces + 1
      • melee.damage + 2
      • melee.cooldown * 0.95
      • melee.knockback + 1
      • melee.heal + 1
      • ammo.max + 1
      • ammo.reloadSpeed * 0.95
  • Bomb Candy (Unused)
    • Creates a small explosion.
Floor Items Floor Items are pickups that give the effect of an Item when collided with. Floor Consumables Floor Consumables are pickups that give the effect of a Consumable when collided with.
