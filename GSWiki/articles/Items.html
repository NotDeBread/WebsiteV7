Items This article is about Items, not to be confused with Power Items or Elixirs. Items are one of three types of items that can appear in the Shop. They give the player permanent bonuses and special abilities.
Items can come in 5 different rarities based on these weights:
  • COMMON
    : 55 (~55%)
  • UNCOMMON
    : 27 (~27%)
  • RARE
    : 16 (~16%)
  • EPIC
    : 1.95 (~2%)
  • MYTHIC
    : 0.01 (~0.01%)
These weights change with the players luck value, shown to the right.
