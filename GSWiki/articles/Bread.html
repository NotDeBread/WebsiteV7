Bread Bread (originally known as DeBread) is the first character in both Goober Shooter games, being the main character in the original Goober Shooter. They are unlocked by default, have no stat changes, and use the Gun. Achievements
  • The rebrand is real: Die while playing as Bread. (Unlocks Fella)
  • Bread Perfection: Defeat The Tutorialist while playing as Bread. (Unlocks The Tophat)
  • Ah! So Spooky!: Defeat The Tutorialist while playing as Bread using only the Spooky! mod. (Unlocks Undead Bread)
