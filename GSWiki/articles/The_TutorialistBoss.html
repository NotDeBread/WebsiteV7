The Tutorialist (Boss) This article is about the Boss not to be confused with the Character, see The Tutorialist. The Tutorialist is the current final boss of Goober Shooter 2. Behavior Phase 1 During phase 1, The Tutorialist will steadily follow the player, firing different kinds of projectiles and sometimes Tutorialist Servants. The Tutorialist deals no contact damage, so you do not need to go out of your way to avoid them.
Phase 2 During this phase The Tutorialist will stay located towards the top of the play area, and start creating area based attacks.
Death Once The Tutorialist reachs 0HP, they create a knockback explosion and fire 17 sequential rings of projectiles for a couple of seconds, then finally exploding. Once killed, they drop a random
MYTHIC
item. Notes
  • The Tutorialist will never spawn a Tutorialist Servant if there is already one on screen. (As of playtest21)
