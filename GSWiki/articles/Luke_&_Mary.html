Luke & Mary Luke & Mary is the only character in Goober Shooter 2 that lets the player control two different character entities. Luke doesn't have a weapon but instead a strong and large melee. Mary follows Luke around and carries the MP5SD, which has high damage, poor accuracy, and bursting rounds. Strategy
  • Always try to buy HP increasing upgrades like Hard Hat, Mega Mushroom, Super Mushroom, and Chicken Alfredo, since Mary's max health is always 50% of the players max health.
  • Be on the lookout for Mary's Epipen in the shop as it is extremely good for keeping Mary alive.
Notes
  • Mary can only take damage from projectiles.
  • Mary's max health is always 50% of the players max health.
  • Mary heals 10% of the players max health every time a wave spawns. This is the only way besides using Mary's Epipen, to heal Mary
  • Additional items that effect Mary can appear in the shop while playing as Luke:
    • Mary Insurance
    • Mary Duplicator
    • Mary's Epipen
