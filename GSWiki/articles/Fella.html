Fella Fella is the second character in Goober Shooter 2 being the featured character of the game. They start with the Poker Chip power item, and use the Sniper weapon. Trivia
  • The achievement that unlocks this character (The rebrand is real) is a reference to how the creator of the game, Fella, rebranded from a fox character, Bread, to their current raccoon character, Fella.
Achievements
  • Fella Perfection: Defeat The Tutorialist while playing as Fella. (Unlocks Raccoon Tail)
