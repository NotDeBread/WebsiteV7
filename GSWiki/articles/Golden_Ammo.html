Golden Ammo Golden Ammo is a strong Item that gives projectiles a chance to spawn a random coin when hitting an enemy. Higher value coins have a lower chance of spawning, using these weights:
  • 100 (~56% chance): Copper Coin ($1)
  • 50 (~28% chance): Silver Coin ($5)
  • 20 (~11% chance): Gold Coin ($10)
  • 5 (~3% chance): Emerald Coin ($25)
  • 1 (~0.6% chance): Diamond Coin ($100)
Synergies
  • Items that let player projectiles hit enemies multiple times like Screw, Drill Ammo, and Ghost Ammo are very powerful since every time the projectile hits an enemy, it can create a coin.
  • Lucky Penny can duplicate pickups created by Golden Ammo.
Notes
  • If there are more than 100 pickups on screen, golden projectiles will no longer spawn coins but instead add them straight to the players money. This is put in place to avoid lag.
  • Having a coinChance value more and 100% can cause the projectile to create multiple coins at once. (1 guaranteed for every 100%)
History
  • playtest8: Added Golden Ammo.
  • playtest22: Golden Ammo now gives an increased chance to spawn a coin (+1% -> +2%).
