Money Bonuses Once the shop opens after a set of waves, the player can gain various different Money Bonuses.
Bonus Details
Max health bonus Gives $25 if the player is at max health.
Flawless Gives $50 + the current wave if the player survives all waves since the previous shop without getting hit once.
Interest Gives $1 for every $5 kept when leaving the previous shop.
This is limited by the players interestLimit value, which can be increased with upgrades such as Credit Card.
Boss kill Gives $25 for each boss the player has killed since the previous shop.
Store credit Gives money equal to the players couponBonus value, which can be increased with the Store Credit item.
Money Trough Gives money equal to 2x the player's wave for each Money trough the player has that is alive.
Interactions
  • The player can still gain money bonuses in no-money challenges like Poverty and Greed.
  • These bonuses still apply in the Sandbox.
Modding To give the player a money bonus, append an object to the player.moneyBonusQueue array using this format: 
player.moneyBonusQueue.push({
    text: 'Example', //The name that is displayed
    value: 25, //The amount of money that is given to the player
})
Keep in mind, these bonuses only apply once the player enters the shop.
