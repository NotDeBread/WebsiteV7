Incendiary Ammo Incendiary Ammo is a Item that gives projectiles a chance to set hit enemies on fire. Enemies that are on fire take 1 damage every tick. Notes
  • The chance whether a projectile is 'firey' or not is determined when the projectile is shot, which is unlike most upgrades where thier ability is rolled when hitting an enemy.
